## Step 1 — `/launcher` on `docs/prd/benchmark-suite.md`, **Phase 2 (cont. II)**

Per-case **process isolation** in `ltlf-ek-bench`. Full spec and green checkpoint
are in that PRD's *Implementation phases* list; do not re-derive them here.

In one line: `RunCaseWithTimeout` (`src/ltlf_ek_bench.cpp:283`) **detaches** its
worker on a deadline miss, the detached worker is still inside Spot/MONA at
process exit, and so **any** timing-out case ends the run in SIGSEGV *before*
`--out` is written. Replace it with a forked child per case — `fork()`, measure
in the child, return over a pipe, `waitpid` on the deadline, `SIGKILL` and reap
on a miss.

**This is the day's blocking dependency.** Steps 2 and 3 are both downstream of
it; if step 1 stops, the day stops with it.

**Watch for:** a child that dies by signal, times out, or OOMs must still yield a
*row* — absent metrics, never zeros (B2 rule 1). Do not let the parent inherit
the child's Spot/BDD state.

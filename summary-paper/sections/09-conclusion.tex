\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
\guide{One paragraph: problem, approach, main result, next step.}

We studied $\LTLf$ synthesis in which external transducers $\Tin$ and $\Tout$ fix some inputs and outputs, and solved it with NFA, DFA and on-the-fly products of the goal automaton with the transducers, which agree on every benchmark and with \tool{ltlfsynt}.
Knowledge pays off when folding it into the formula is expensive, up to $24\times$ on \texttt{co-generated} and over $100\times$ on \texttt{slippery-*}, but those are especially made examples that couldnt be found in the wild.
